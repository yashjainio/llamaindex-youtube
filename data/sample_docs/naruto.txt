Naruto — Series Overview

Overview
Naruto is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Masashi Kishimoto, serialized in Weekly Shonen Jump from 1999 to 2014. The story follows Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja from the Hidden Leaf Village who dreams of becoming Hokage, the village's strongest and most respected leader, despite being shunned by many villagers for reasons he doesn't initially understand.

Protagonist and abilities
Naruto Uzumaki is the jinchuriki of Kurama, the Nine-Tailed Fox, a powerful being sealed inside him as an infant, which is the real reason for the villagers' fear and hostility. His signature technique is the Rasengan, a swirling ball of concentrated chakra taught to him by Jiraiya, one of the legendary Sannin. He's also famous for the Shadow Clone Jutsu (Kage Bunshin no Jutsu), which he uses far more liberally than most ninja due to his unusually large chakra reserves — a technique most ninja avoid overusing because each clone splits the user's chakra. Later in the series, Naruto learns Sage Mode, drawing on natural energy from Mount Myoboku, and eventually gains direct access to Kurama's chakra as a full partner rather than a sealed prisoner.

Story arcs
The Chunin Exams arc introduces most of Naruto's generation of ninja and establishes the political tension between hidden villages. The Pain's Assault arc is a major turning point, where Naruto confronts Pain (secretly controlled by his former mentor's student, Nagato) after Pain destroys the Hidden Leaf Village, and Naruto's restraint in victory becomes a defining character moment. The series culminates in the Fourth Great Ninja War arc, where the world's shinobi forces unite against Madara Uchiha and the resurrected Ten-Tails.

Production
Naruto was animated by Studio Pierrot. The original Naruto anime aired from 2002 to 2007 (220 episodes), followed by Naruto: Shippuden from 2007 to 2017 (500 episodes), covering the manga's time-skip storyline. The English dub was produced by Viz Media and helped introduce a generation of Western viewers to shonen anime in the mid-2000s.

Legacy and cultural impact
Naruto is frequently grouped with One Piece and Bleach as one of the "Big Three" of Weekly Shonen Jump's 2000s lineup, a trio credited with driving anime's mainstream popularity outside Japan. The series has sold over 250 million copies of the manga worldwide, and its influence extends into fighting games, a long-running spin-off (Boruto), and widespread use of its terminology (like "believe it" and "Dattebayo") in broader pop culture.

Themes
Beyond ninja combat, Naruto is fundamentally a story about found family, perseverance despite social rejection, and breaking cycles of hatred passed down between generations — a theme made explicit through the parallel between Naruto and his eventual rival-turned-friend Sasuke Uchiha, both of whom lost their families to forces largely outside their control.

Supporting characters
Sasuke Uchiha is Naruto's teammate and eventual rival, driven by a need to avenge his clan and surpass his older brother Itachi, who is later revealed to have killed their family under tragic, morally complicated orders. Sakura Haruno starts as a relatively weak teammate but trains under the Sannin Tsunade to become a skilled medical ninja. Kakashi Hatake, their team leader, is known for the Sharingan eye implanted in his socket and his signature Chidori technique. Jiraiya, one of the legendary Sannin and Naruto's mentor, authors the novel series that inspires Naruto's own eventual title and dies gathering intelligence on the Akatsuki's leader, Pain.

Trivia
Naruto's original run and Shippuden together total 720 television episodes, making it one of the longest-running mainstream shonen adaptations. The franchise has produced numerous theatrical films and spawned the sequel series Boruto: Naruto Next Generations, following Naruto's son. The word "dattebayo," Naruto's verbal tic, has no direct English translation and was handled differently across various dub localizations.

Voice cast
Naruto Uzumaki is voiced by Junko Takeuchi in the original Japanese dub, a role she has held across the entire franchise including video games and later Boruto appearances. In the English dub produced by Viz Media, Naruto is voiced by Maile Flanagan. Sasuke Uchiha is voiced by Noriaki Sugiyama in Japanese and Yuri Lowenthal in English, both of whom likewise reprised their roles consistently across the franchise's many spin-offs and films.

Where to watch
Naruto and Naruto: Shippuden are widely available on Crunchyroll and Hulu in most English-speaking territories, alongside VIZ Media's own streaming platform. The manga is available digitally through VIZ Media's Shonen Jump subscription service, which also carries most other major Weekly Shonen Jump titles referenced across this document collection.
