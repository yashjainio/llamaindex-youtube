Demon Slayer — Series Overview

Overview
Demon Slayer (Kimetsu no Yaiba) is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Koyoharu Gotouge, serialized in Weekly Shonen Jump from 2016 to 2020. Set in Taisho-era Japan, it follows Tanjiro Kamado, a kind-hearted boy who becomes a demon slayer after his family is slaughtered by a demon and his younger sister Nezuko is turned into one herself, though she retains her humanity and fights alongside him.

Protagonist and abilities
Tanjiro joins the Demon Slayer Corps to find a cure for Nezuko and avenge his family. He trains under former Hashira (top-ranking slayer) Sakonji Urokodaki and initially fights using Water Breathing, a swordsmanship style that mimics the fluid, adaptable properties of water. Later in the series, Tanjiro awakens Hinokami Kagura, also known as Sun Breathing, the original breathing style from which all other styles descended, tied to his family's inherited dance ritual. Nezuko, meanwhile, develops unique demon abilities of her own, including Blood Demon Art techniques that produce explosive, fire-like effects, while somehow resisting the urge to feed on human blood that afflicts other demons.

Story arcs
The Final Selection arc tests aspiring demon slayers against demons on a mountain, establishing the stakes of the Corps' training. The Mount Natagumo arc introduces Tanjiro's first major team-based battle against a demon family and features the Hashira Giyu Tomioka. The Entertainment District arc, featuring Hashira Tengen Uzui, is widely considered a high point of the series for its animation and character writing. The Infinity Castle arc escalates into the final confrontation with the series' primary antagonist, Muzan Kibutsuji, the original demon from whom all others descend.

Production
Demon Slayer is animated by ufotable, a studio known for blending traditional 2D animation with CGI backgrounds and effects to striking effect, particularly in fight choreography. The Mugen Train arc was adapted into a theatrical film, Demon Slayer: Mugen Train, which became the highest-grossing Japanese film of all time upon its 2020 release, driven partly by pandemic-era theater conditions in Japan and the series' surging popularity.

Legacy and cultural impact
Demon Slayer is widely credited with driving a renewed mainstream anime boom in the early 2020s, both in Japan and internationally, comparable to the impact Dragon Ball Z and Naruto had in earlier decades. Its combination of traditional shonen structure with unusually high production values set a new bar for what audiences expected from anime adaptations of ongoing manga.

Themes
Demon Slayer explores grief and compassion in tandem — Tanjiro's defining trait is that he extends empathy even to the demons he's forced to kill, understanding many of them as tragic figures rather than pure villains, which sets the series apart from more black-and-white good-versus-evil framing common in the genre.

Supporting characters
Zenitsu Agatsuma fights using a single, extraordinarily refined form of Thunder Breathing, Thunderclap and Flash, but is otherwise cowardly and only able to perform at his full potential while unconscious or terrified. Inosuke Hashibira, raised by boars in the mountains, fights with dual serrated blades and a self-taught, unconventional breathing style called Beast Breathing. Giyu Tomioka, the Water Hashira, is the demon slayer who spares Nezuko's life and sets Tanjiro on his path to joining the Corps. Shinobu Kocho, the Insect Hashira, uses poison-coated blades since her physical strength is lower than most Hashira, compensating with speed and precision instead.

Trivia
Demon Slayer: Mugen Train became the highest-grossing Japanese film of all time upon release in 2020, a record it held for several years afterward. ufotable's distinctive visual style for the series' fight scenes, blending hand-drawn animation with CGI effects for techniques like Hinokami Kagura, has been widely imitated by other studios since. The franchise has continued with additional theatrical films covering later story arcs rather than reverting to a purely episodic television format.

Voice cast
Tanjiro Kamado is voiced by Natsuki Hanae in the original Japanese dub. In the English dub produced by Aniplex of America, Tanjiro is voiced by Zach Aguilar. Nezuko Kamado is voiced by Akari Kito in Japanese and Abby Trott in English — a notably challenging role for both actresses since Nezuko communicates almost entirely through muffled vocalizations rather than full dialogue for much of the series.

Where to watch
Demon Slayer is available on Crunchyroll, and the Mugen Train film has had both theatrical re-releases and home streaming availability following its record-breaking Japanese box office run. The manga is available digitally through VIZ Media, and ufotable has continued releasing subsequent story arcs as theatrical films rather than a traditional television season format.
